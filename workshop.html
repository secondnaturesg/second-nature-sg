Nature in the Classroom

Bring nature-based learning to accessible indoor spaces using prepared plant materials and simple equipment.

On-site workshops
Nature-based learning indoors

Bring the living world into the learning space.

A classroom or community room can become a place for hands-on nature learning with prepared plants, materials and simple activities.

Nature in the Classroom brings selected aspects of nature-based learning indoors where an outdoor visit may not be suitable. Sessions can include observation, sorting, making, planting or basic investigations.

Activities are planned around the space, age group and learning aims. We aim to preserve curiosity and participant choice while working within indoor safety and hygiene requirements.

What a session can include

01

Observe and compare

Examine prepared leaves, seeds and plants through simple guided prompts.

02

Make and investigate

Use safe materials for small experiments or nature-related making.

03

Plant and care

Try age-appropriate planting or care tasks with suitable equipment.

04

Share learning

Use drawings, questions, group discussion or demonstrations to describe observations.

Examples of activities

Activities are selected and adjusted to suit the group and programme objectives.

  • Plant part exploration: Look closely at stems, leaves, roots and seeds using prepared specimens.
  • Nature sorting: Group materials by visible features such as size, shape, texture or colour.
  • Mini science investigation: Explore water, floating, light or plant-related phenomena with suitable controls.
  • Seed sowing: Prepare a container, sow seeds and discuss future plant care.
  • Nature-inspired making: Use prepared natural items for a simple piece of work or group display.
  • Question and reflection: Share what participants noticed and what they would like to investigate next.

What participants may gain

  • Access nature-related learning without needing an outdoor venue.
  • Practise observation, sorting, comparing and asking questions.
  • Engage with plant materials in an organised setting.
  • Build confidence through suitable hands-on tasks.
  • Support social learning and shared discussion.
  • Connect indoor observations with the wider natural world.

Suitable for different groups

  • Preschool groups: Short sensory and play-based activities with close supervision.
  • Primary classes: Curriculum-linked investigations and hands-on learning.
  • Youth groups: More detailed questions, collaborative tasks and small projects.
  • Care and community centres: Adapted plant activities and supported participation.

How we plan the session

We bring suitable materials and basic equipment to an accessible room. The organiser provides agreed furniture, working space, access and supervision.

Some activities may require water, plants or soil, while others use dry materials. We agree on hygiene and cleanup arrangements before the session.

What we need before the programme

To plan an indoor nature session, organisers should share:

  • Number and age range of participants
  • Learning goals and suitable activity themes
  • Room size, tables, seating and ventilation
  • Water access, washing and cleaning arrangements
  • Staff support and supervision arrangements
  • Relevant allergies, sensory and access needs
  • Restrictions on soil, live plants or materials
  • Time available, setup access and cleanup needs
Participation is by invitation, not pressure. Participants may observe rather than handle materials, use adjusted tools or complete fewer steps. Facilitation is adapted to the group.

Caring for people and nature

We review material suitability, small parts, soil and water handling, tool use, ventilation and cleaning arrangements.

An indoor nature session cannot fully replace observing an ecosystem in place, but can provide useful opportunities for learning and engagement.

Programme scope

Nature in the Classroom is an educational and community activity. It is not presented as clinical treatment or a full Forest School programme.
