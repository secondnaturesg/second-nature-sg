Consultancy & capacity building

Support beyond the session itself.

Alongside direct programmes, Second Nature can support organisations with activity planning, nature-space ideas and facilitator development. Each scope is discussed before commitments are confirmed.

Where we can support

🌿

Nature space planning

Ideas for how a garden, green corner or naturalised space could support accessible participation and learning.

📚

Curriculum & programme design

Structure age-appropriate learning aims, activity sequences, materials, observation and reflection.

👥

Facilitator development

Training plans around inquiry-led learning, participant choice, inclusive support and nature-related risk planning.

📋

Safety & operating guidance

Support the design of checklists, suitability screening, basic site reviews and programme documentation.

Potential areas of work

  • Plant- and nature-based engagement in senior activity centres and care settings.
  • Nature learning for schools, preschools and youth programmes.
  • Accessible gardening and hands-on activities in shared spaces.
  • Training volunteers and staff on activity facilitation and suitable support.
  • Evaluating participant engagement and outcomes in programme pilots.
Where a project involves clinical care, specialised rehabilitation, a regulated activity or formal landscape design, the partner may need an appropriately qualified professional. Any such role will be agreed separately.

Our process

01

Consult

Understand participants, the site and the organisation's objectives.

02

Scope

Agree on deliverables, professional roles, materials, access and timeline.

03

Support

Prepare resources or workshops and work with the partner team.

04

Review

Gather feedback, document recommendations and agree any follow-up work.
