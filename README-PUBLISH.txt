IMPORTANT PRE-PUBLICATION CHECKS
1. Contact email: currently set to hello@thegoodearthschool.org, a public address supplied in earlier TGES material. Confirm it is the right receiving mailbox for Second Nature. To change it, search all files for this address, including assets/js/main.js.
2. Outcomes: 2,600+ reached and 83% reporting feeling more positive were supplied for this website. Add the feedback sample, time period and question wording if known. We do not claim a clinical effect.
3. Dates: April 2024 beginning, April and June 2025 grant support, and March 2026 rebrand are as supplied. No grants or partners are named without confirmation.
4. Professional practice: the copy distinguishes therapy-informed programmes from clinical therapy or a certified Forest School. It does not claim credentials or accreditation.
5. Verify the 10 safety commitments against your actual operating procedures and insurer/site rules before presenting them as established practice.
6. The header uses a text wordmark and a new original SVG leaf symbol. Replace these with your final approved logo if you have one.
7. Site uses no backend. The enquiry form opens the visitor's email client, so its completion depends on the visitor having one configured. For direct submissions, connect a form provider later.
8. No prices, phone numbers, unverified team bios, partner logos or social URLs were invented.
9. Original decorative SVG artwork ships locally. Two backgrounds also attempt to load free Unsplash-hosted forest/plant images for photographic texture when online. The local artwork remains underneath if offline. If you prefer all images to be locally hosted, replace the images.unsplash.com URLs in assets/css/main.css with your own licensed images and upload those files.
10. Template: section structure adapted from the supplied BootstrapMade 'Instant' HTML (header, hero, about, features, services, service-details, sidebar and footer). The original upload did not contain the assets folder, so all styles and scripts were recreated locally to make the package work independently. Keep the BootstrapMade credit; review their license if replacing or extending code.
